LumiPen is a Filipino-made lifestyle and everyday-carry brand that creates practical tools for students, workers, travelers and busy individuals. Our flagship product, the LumiPen Multifunctional Pen, combines a smooth-writing pen, built-in flashlight, emergency whistle and keychain attachment in one compact design.

LumiPen was created with one simple idea: people should not need to carry several separate tools just to stay prepared. Whether you are taking notes in class, walking home at night, searching for something in a dark bag, or traveling to a new place, LumiPen gives you a reliable everyday companion.

What makes LumiPen unique

LumiPen is more than an ordinary pen. It combines three practical functions (writing, lighting and emergency signaling) into one compact product. Unlike bulky emergency tools, it is lightweight, portable and suitable for everyday use, and its colors let you choose a style that fits your personality.

What you get

  • Writing pen for notes, schoolwork, forms and everyday writing
  • LED pen light for power interruptions, travel and low light
  • Emergency whistle to attract attention when help is needed
  • Keychain attachment for bags, ID laces, keys or belts
  • Colors: Black, White, Pink, Purple and Blue

Who LumiPen is for

  • Students who need a reliable pen and flashlight for school
  • Office workers who want a practical everyday tool
  • Commuters and travelers who prefer compact essentials
  • Outdoor enthusiasts who need a portable light and whistle
  • Gift buyers looking for useful and affordable presents
  • Anyone who wants a simple everyday safety accessory

Our vision

To become a trusted Filipino everyday-carry brand known for useful, durable and accessible multifunctional products.

Our mission

To make everyday preparedness simple, affordable and stylish, with practical tools that help people stay ready for school, work, travel and unexpected situations.

Business goals

  • Provide high-quality multifunctional pens at student-friendly prices
  • Build a recognizable brand in the everyday-carry and personal-safety accessories market
  • Expand LumiPen to schools, offices, convenience stores and online marketplaces
  • Create useful content that helps customers choose practical everyday tools
  • Deliver excellent customer service before, during and after every purchase
